This repository contains a focused derivative of stable-worldmodel and LeWM by Lucas Maes and collaborators. Their MIT copyright and permission notice is retained in LICENSE. DualWM-specific changes include temporal abstraction, variational macro actions, staged training and hierarchical planning.

Upstream projects: https://github.com/galilai-group/stable-worldmodel and https://github.com/galilai-group/le-wm.

OGBench and dm_control are external dependencies with their own licenses. Model and dataset distribution are separate from the source package. Checkpoint configurations retain the stable_worldmodel namespace for compatibility with existing weights.
